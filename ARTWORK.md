# Book 9 artwork

Seven portrait watercolor illustrations form the starter reading edition: one cover and six interior scenes. They follow the established Waffles & Pip books and are designed to be reused across the 30 manuscript pages.

## Character continuity

- Waffles is the fluffy white rabbit with pink inner ears and nose, expressive brown eyes, and one slightly floppy ear.
- Pip is the tiny round bright-blue songbird with a small orange-yellow beak and feet.
- Charlie is the cheerful small pink piglet with floppy ears, curly tail, and a few muddy patches.
- Lily has big brown eyes and brown hair in a high bun. Her bright purple farm boots remain visible in winter.
- Grandma has a kind face, silver-white bun, and blue floral clothing.
- Cracker is based on the user-supplied photograph of the real Cracker: a large blue-and-gold macaw with blue wings and back, golden-yellow chest, greenish forehead, white facial skin with black feather lines, a large black beak, and a long blue tail. He has no red feathers and must remain clearly different from Pip.

## Files and scene prompts

- `cover.png`: Waffles and Pip find a silver bell on a snowy clue trail leading toward the decorated red barn. Exact title and Book 9 subtitle are printed on the cover.
- `bell-trail.png`: winter morning; Waffles jumps back from the newly uncovered bell while Pip looks skeptical; red ribbon and cookie crumbs lead onward.
- `friends-trail.png`: Waffles holds the green mitten while Pip, Mouse, Squirrel, Mole, Hazel, and Tilly gather around the golden ornament; Mole wears half a wreath.
- `charlie-reunion.png`: Waffles and Charlie collide joyfully in soft snow at the decorated farm fence while Pip watches.
- `farm-meeting.png`: Lily in her purple boots and Grandma meet Waffles and Pip beside Charlie; real-Cracker plumage and scale are preserved.
- `tree-decorating.png`: Waffles balances safely on Charlie to hang a red ornament while Lily, Pip, Grandma, and Cracker decorate the tree.
- `christmas-together.png`: Lily sits with Waffles and Pip beside the glowing tree while Grandma, Cracker, Charlie, and the woodland friends gather nearby. Waffles's mistakenly generated scarf was removed to preserve manuscript continuity.

All interior prompts specified portrait 2:3 composition, richly textured luminous watercolor, full ears and bodies inside safe margins, child-safe humor, no embedded story text, no watermarks, and strict separation between tiny Pip and large macaw Cracker.
